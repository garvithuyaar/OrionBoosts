Questions

Quick answers before you buy.

Why only even numbers of boosts?

Boosts are sold in pairs, so you can pick any even amount from 2 to 30.

What is the difference between 1M and 3M?

1M boosts last 1 month and 3M boosts last 3 months. 1M costs $0.27 and 3M costs $0.88 per 2 boosts.

Do you need my Discord password?

Never. We only need your server invite link.

How do I pay?

Pay in Litecoin (LTC). Send the exact amount shown at checkout, then enter your transaction ID.

When does delivery start?

As soon as your payment is confirmed on the network.
